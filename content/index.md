## Explore Middle-Earth

Middle-earth is more than just a fictional fantasy setting—it is a deeply rich linguistic and historical mythos spanning multiple ages:

* **The First Age**: The rebellion of the Noldor, the Silmarils, and the war against Morgoth.
* **The Second Age**: The rise and fall of the island kingdom of Númenor and the forging of the Rings of Power.
* **The Third Age**: The quest to destroy the One Ring and the re-establishment of the King of Gondor.
