# About J.R.R. Tolkien

**John Ronald Reuel Tolkien** (1892 – 1973) was an English writer, poet, philologist, and academic. He was the Rawlinson and Bosworth Professor of Anglo-Saxon and a Fellow of Pembroke College, Oxford.

## The Inklings

Tolkien was a close friend of **C.S. Lewis** (author of *The Chronicles of Narnia*), and together they were leading members of the informal Oxford literary discussion group known as the *Inklings*.

> "If more of us valued food and cheer and song above hoarded gold, it would be a merrier world."
> — *Thorin Oakenshield*

### Timeline of Publications

* **1937**: Publication of *The Hobbit*
* **1954**: Publication of *The Fellowship of the Ring* and *The Two Towers*
* **1955**: Publication of *The Return of the King*
* **1977**: Posthumous publication of *The Silmarillion*, edited by his son Christopher Tolkien
